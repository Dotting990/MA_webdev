How to slove a 3x3 Rubik's cube
(For beginers)

This is a tutorial for beginers on how to solve a 3x3 Rubik's cube.
If your cube is already scrabled then we can move onto the next step.
If not, scramble your cube.

The next step is to learn the peices on the cube. First is the corner peices A standared Rubik's cube has 8 corner peices. The corner peices are well the corners of the cube. They have 3 colors each. Next is the edges. There are 12 edge pecies on a standared 3x3. They have 2 colors on each edge peice. Finaly are the centers. The centers are the colors in the middle of each side of the cube. They show you where each color goes. Like the blue side would go on the side where the blue center is. Also a Rubik's cube is solved layer by layer NOT side by side

Now that you have a scrambled cube, know the peices, and know how a Rubik's cube is solved we can get to solving. First step is to start with a white cross. A white cross is where we match all the edges with white to the white center and the red, blue, orange, and green centers. First find a edge with white and move it to the white center and make sure the white part of the edge touches the white part. Now, look at the other color on the edge and match it with its coresponding color so that color matches with its coresponding colored center and the white part matches with the white center.
