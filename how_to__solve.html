How to slove a 3x3 Rubik's cube
(For beginers)

This is a tutorial for beginers on how to solve a 3x3 Rubik's cube.
If your cube is already scrabled then we can move onto the next step.
If not, scramble your cube.

The next step is to learn the peices on the cube. First is the corner peices A standared Rubik's cube has

8 corner peices
The corner peices are well the corners of the cube. They have 3 colors each
12 edge pecies
Edges have 2 colors on each edge peice
4 centers
colors in the middle of each side of the cube. They show you where each color goes.

A Rubik's cube is solved layer by layer NOT side by side

Now that you have a scrambled cube, know the peices, and know how a Rubik's cube is solved we can get to solving.

First step

start with a white cross. A white cross is where we match all the edges with white to the white center and the red, blue, orange, and green centers. First find a edge with white and move it to the white center and make sure the white part of the edge touches the white part. Now, look at the other color on the edge and match it with its coresponding color so that color matches with its coresponding colored center and the white part matches with the white center. Do that for every edge with white on it. So, it should look like this

For example if i wanted to solve a coner for red and blue i would find a coner with Blue, red, and white and put it where it is above the white center and between the red and blue center and to the algorithems.
